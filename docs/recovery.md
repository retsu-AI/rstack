# Recover without duplicate effects

First preserve the original request, current approval and holds, owner, branch/head, dirty files, process/task identity, operation ID, returned error, timestamps and artifact hashes. A canceled tool request does not prove that a human canceled it or that no side effect occurred. An interrupted task does not erase valid artifacts.

Use supported read-only status and destination readback for the specific operation. For a send, upload, create, payment or publication with uncertain outcome, look for its actual effect under the original identity. A found effect is reconciled once; do not send it again. An absent readback is evidence only within its stated scope. Inconclusive readback keeps the action uncertain. Reuse the same idempotency identity when a supported retry is authorized.

A permission denial is a stop for that affected action. Record the denial and relevant scope; do not route around it with another provider, account, credential, browser or renamed operation. Do not retry a successful action or an explicit denial. For a retryable transient failure, name the failure class, finite attempt limit, backoff and resumption evidence. A deterministic failure needs a changed cause before retry. Inspect actual side effects before resuming or replacing any owner.

Restore the consolidated brief before authorized continuation. A stop/hold requires actual current resume authority. Preserve unrelated work; no destructive reset, stash overwrite or broad cleanup. Remove only owned disposable outputs after checking they are no longer needed. Keep sources, uncommitted edits, receipts, review records and release artifacts. Cleanup is not a reason to change global security or terminate unrelated services.

Preserve an existing plugin's established responsibility. Rstack does not reinstall plugins or claim runtime enforcement. When a recurring failure can be prevented in the product's types, architecture, lint or CI, propose the smallest authorized structural fix before adding another prose rule.

Restore [the native goal and obligation inventory](completion-contract.md) alongside artifact state. An unknown or conflicting goal cannot be treated as absent. Recovery completion applies to its supporting outcome, not the unknown original delivery.

Restore [the ordered plan](execution-plan.md), current W/V assignments, exact accepted subjects, pending findings and remaining loop budget. Reconcile surviving activation or remote jobs before replacement. Retain verifier defects as defects rather than relabeling a product failure; repair and independently review the changed verifier before relying on affected results.
